Slowcooker Curried Sausages

Ingredients

  • Beef Sausages
  • Chopped Potatoes
  • Sliced Carrot
  • Chopped Onion
  • 1 Package of Japanese Curry
  • 2 cups of boiled water

for the veggies and sausages, the amounts don't really matter, just use whatever you have or how much you like

Method

  1. Put everything in the slow cooker
  2. boil around 2 cups of water
  3. break up the japanese curry and stir it into 1 cup of the boiling water
  4. when its nice and thick, add some of the remaining cup of water until its the right level of thickness for you (not too watery)
  5. Pour it into the slow cooker and stir everything up to make sure its well coated in the curry mix
  6. cook for around 6 hours on high (may need a bit longer depending on how chunky your veggies are)

Notes

  • I use S&B Golden Curry Mix (Mild). The curry comes in a block of around 5 cubes/fingers and you break it up to dissolve in water. It should be available in many countries, but in case its not, use whatever curry mix you like as an alternative, or google "Japanese Curry mix" and you'll get some recipes.
