Channel Recs

Kareem Rahma
Kareem has a series called 'Keep the Meter Running' where he jumps in a taxi and keeps the meter running while he spends the day (or longer) with the taxi driver, exploring their city and the places/history that are important to the driver
Jet Lag: The Game
Super fun travel channel where a group of friends travel to different countries playing tag, hide and seek, capture the flag etc across an entire country. They utilise a deck of cards they have created (and sell) that have challenges and curses to help or hinder them as they travel.
Tara Farms
Tara is a third generation sheep farmer raising her own sheep for meat. She makes hilarious youtube videos about daily farm life on her 4000 acre family farm in Victoria, Australia.
