loosley inspired by the /now page movement, a /now page is a page where you write about what you are up to "now" and so this is my version of it.

What I am up to in August 2026

Playing Sort Them Ducks

Sort Them Ducks is a cozy organisation game where you are in a room with over 4000 rubber ducks and you need to sort and place all the ducks onto the right shelves. So many ducks! There are upgrades you can unlock to make things a little easier, like increased inventory space, being able to throw them from a distance, automatically pick up ducks that match your hand etc. Its lots of fun!

Rereading and Archiving Castle Fanfic

I've been rereading some Castle fanfic, and making an archive of my fav fics on my computer so I always have them. Most older Castle Fanfic is on Fanfiction do net and it has been down a decent number of times recently, and it suddenly occured to me that I could lose my favs, so I downloaded them all as epubs using Fichub which lets you download fics from a number of archives. I'll keep a copy of them all on my computer and also on my ipad. Maybe even throw a zip file into dropbox.

Being obsessed over Obsession

I saw the horror movie Obsession earlier this month and omg I think its probably my horror movie of the year. I've only seen it once all the way through although I do plan to rewatch it soon, but I have been watching a TON of reaction videos on youtube which basically recap (via clips) the whole movie, so if you count those I have probably seen Obsession 50 times now. Its such a well done movie, and everyone is so good in it. And its layered. From the dialogue to the way it was shot. Definitely worth a watch if you haven't seen it already.

Interview With The Vampire Season 3 - The Vampire Lestat

Hooooly shirtballs, I LOVE SEASON 4. I did enjoy the previous seasons, those are pretty good, but The Vampire Lestat just ROCKS MY WORLD. Not sure what it is about it. But I am obsessed. OBSESSED! I think partially its 1980s rockstar Lestast that speaks to my teen self who was totally into rock in the 80s, partially just a really good story. But whatever it was, this season was probably my fav TV show this year. Sam Reid singing sounds so much like some of my fav music. Nick Cave (and the bad seeds), Jim Morrison (the doors), Neil Hannon (The Divine Comedy), etc. I am hoping they bring the Lestat album out on Vinyl. I would love to own that!
