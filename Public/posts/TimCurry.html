RIP Tim Curry

August 27, 2026
Tags: movie

So today I woke up to the news that Tim Curry had passed away.

Tim Curry was an actor who played a variety of roles of the course of career, which spanned from the 1970s to 2026. He was born in England but lived in LA for most of his career.

I first heard of Tim Curry when I was around 10 years old, sometime in the 1980s through the movie Rocky Horror Picture Show.

I used to go to Blue Light Discos as a kid (they were drug-free/alcohol-free discos held by local police for kids under 16) and one of the songs they would always play was The Time Warp which fast became my favourite song. I found out it was from a movie and somehow I was allowed to rent it from my local vhs rental shop.

And so I watched Rocky Horror. And I did not like it (then). lol

I mean, I was ten and this wasn't really a movie for young kids. I have no idea how I was allowed to watch except my parents weren't very present.

I next saw Tim Curry probably in Annie, as Rooster. I loved Annie, it is still today one of my favourite movies. I didn't really like the character of Rooster as a kid but I think he is pretty cool now as an adult.

From there I probably saw him in a few other movies, but I doubt at any time I would have been able to name him, he would have just been an actor that was in movies I liked.

Then when I was around 18, I saw him in Stephen King's IT, a miniseries based on his book. Tim Curry was cast as Pennywise the Clown, and oh boy I loved Pennywise! He was so scary but also super funny. The way he spoke and the way his lip curled made him a terrifying clown. Tim Curry played the role so well.

Not long after that I rewatched Rocky Horror Picture Show and fell in love with it. The songs were so good, and the plot was hilarious. Tim Curry played Frank N Furter and he was SO FREAKING GOOD as Frank.

And of course I loved Tim Curry in other things. As Long John Silver in Muppet Treasure Island, the Cardinal in Three Muskateers, Nigel Thornberry in The Wild Thornerries (which I watched when my child was young). He was also in Home Alone 2, Legend, and more.

In 2012 Tim Curry had a stroke that left him partially paralyzed, affecting the left side of his body and his ability to walk. He also had brain surgery at the time.

But he continued to work, mostly did a lot of voice work in cartoons, and appeared in some Rocky Horror specials.

In 2025 Time Curry did an interview with Ben Mankiewicz on CBS Mornings. When asked if he feared death Tim Curry said:

No, I don’t fear death. I try to avoid it, as I think we all do! But I suspect that in the end, I will welcome it. I think it may be very comforting to go bye-bye, and I want to earn it! I want to earn the privilege of not being here. I think I might very well embrace it."

He died August 25th 2026.
