Free Stuff For Kids Nostalgia

May 2, 2026
Tags: books, nostalgia

Free Stuff For Kids Books

Waayy back in the day when I was a kid, one of my favourite things in the world were the (Australian Edition of) Free Stuff For Kids books by Scott Riddle.

These were small square books filled with things you could write to organisations to get for free. Things like stickers, posters, education packs, etc. It was really just promotional stuff, but as a kid getting mail and free posters and stickers was kinda neat.

The books had instructions on how to send away for the free items, by writing a formal letter, requesting the item listed, and including your mail adress.

There were tons of categories, everything from Fruit and Vegetables, to Power, Environment, Communications, Mining, Food and Nature. They were nearly always skewed towards educational subjects, the sort of thing you would use for school projects. Most were just marketing materials.

Here are some example pages from the book, they each give information about the topic and what you might recieve, along with the adress you needed to write to, and what to ask for.

I took the adress out of the one above because the adress was a physical location and not a PO Box, and the adress still exists but it is no longer the Rural Water Commision.

As an adult now, I can see that some of the items were probably not really all that interesting, I think part of the fun as a kid, was just getting mail. When you're a kid and especially in the time before the internet existed getting mail adressed to you was pretty exciting.

I think I sent away for nearly everything in each edition of the book. I'd sit at my desk and flip through the book to find something interesting and then write my letter. Some of them you needed to include stamped return envelope so they could use it to send the item back in that, but others were completely free.

Not every organisation responded, but quite a lot of them did.

I think I bought every edition for a few years until I grew out it. The books did come out for a few years after that, I know there was one published in 2009. But I was well and truly an adult in 2009!
