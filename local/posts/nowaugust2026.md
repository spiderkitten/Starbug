loosley inspired by the /now page movement, a /now page is a page where you write about what you are up to "now" and so this is my version of it. 

What I am up to in August 2026 

#### Playing Sort Them Ducks 

<a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/4992070/Sort_Them_Ducks/">Sort Them Ducks</a> is a cozy organisation game where you are in a room with over 4000 rubber ducks and you need to sort and place all the ducks onto the right shelves. So many ducks! There are upgrades you can unlock to make things a little easier, like increased inventory space, being able to throw them from a distance, automatically pick up ducks that match your hand etc. Its lots of fun!

#### Being obsessed over Obsession

I saw the horror movie Obsession earlier this month and omg I think its probably my horror movie of the year. I've only seen it once all the way through although I do plan to rewatch it soon, but I have been watching a TON of reaction videos on youtube which basically recap (via clips) the whole movie, so if you count those I have probably seen Obsession 50 times now. Its such a well done movie, and everyone is so good in it. And its <i>layered</i>. From the dialogue to the way it was shot. Definitely worth a watch if you haven't seen it already.
